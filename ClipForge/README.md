# ClipForge

Turns a long video into short vertical clips ready to post, the way Opus Clip does, but everything runs on your own PC.

Paste a YouTube link or drop in a video file. ClipForge then:

1. **Downloads** the video with yt-dlp, at up to 1080p. It uses Firefox cookies automatically if YouTube asks you to sign in.
2. **Transcribes** it with Whisper, including the timing of every word. It uses your NVIDIA GPU (large-v3-turbo) if you have one, or the CPU (small) if not.
3. **Picks the best moments.** A built-in scorer lines up about 30 candidate clips, cut at sentence boundaries. The AI then judges them the way a producer would, tightens the start and end, and writes a hook title and a post caption for each.
   - The AI is **OmniRoute** (`localhost:20128`) first, then **Ollama** directly.
   - If neither is running, the built-in scorer does the whole job.
4. **Reframes** the clip to 9:16. It detects faces, follows whoever is talking by watching which mouth is moving, and holds the frame still between camera cuts.
5. **Edits** the clip:
   - cuts dead air and "um"/"uh"
   - adds punch-in zooms on alternate beats
   - applies a light colour grade
   - cleans up the voice and sets loudness to -14 LUFS
6. **Adds captions with HyperFrames.** Captions are animated word by word, with a hook title card and a progress bar. They're rendered as a transparent layer and laid over the edit. If HyperFrames isn't available, the same design is burned in with ffmpeg.

### Extras

- **Split screen:** when two people share a wide shot and are too far apart for one vertical crop, ClipForge stacks them, one on top and one below, and moves the captions to the seam between them. To turn this off, go to **Settings → Edit → Two people in one shot**.
- **Clips about…:** under **More options**, describe what you want, for example *"his story about the fight"*. The AI ranks clips on that topic first.
- **From / To minute:** only looks for clips in that part of the video.
- **Several links:** put one link per line to queue several videos at once.
- **Score breakdown:** each clip shows Hook, Flow, Value and Trend bars, the same four things Opus Clip scores.
- **Emojis:** an emoji pops above key words. The AI picks them, with a built-in list as a backup.
- **Watermark:** your @handle, shown small at the bottom of each clip, but only if you type one in Settings. It's off by default.
- **No branding:** clips never mention ClipForge or the source channel, and the video file's metadata (title, uploader, link) is wiped. You can post them anywhere.
- **Sound:** a whoosh on each zoom and on the hook title, and a pop on each emoji. These sounds are generated by the app, so there are no licensing issues.
- **Background music:** put your own .mp3 files in `data\music`. Each clip gets a track, which drops in volume automatically whenever someone talks.
- **Caption position:** Auto (just below the face), Top, Middle or Bottom. You set it on the home screen or in **Settings**. The other graphics move out of the captions' way.
- **Caption styles:** there are 10: Bold, Box, Highlight, Pop, Karaoke, Neon, Glitch, Slam, Burst and Clean. Their motion is based on the caption components in the HyperFrames registry.
- **Hook title styles:** Card, Slam (the title slams in and shakes) or Type (typed out with a cursor).
- **Motion graphics:**
  - when someone says a number like "$50,000" or "30 days", a stat card counts it up on screen
  - key words get a hand-drawn underline
  - each zoom gets a light flash
- **Shader transitions:** these are the 14 WebGL transitions from HyperFrames: zoom blur, whip pan, light leak, flash, glitch, chromatic split, swirl, ripple, warp dissolve, lens, warp morph, burn, heat haze and iris. They're used on some of the jump cuts and zoom-ins, with space between them so the clip doesn't look like a template.
  - **Subtle** (the default) uses the four gentle ones, about every 8 seconds.
  - **Bold** uses all 14, about every 5 seconds.
  - You can also pick a single transition in **Settings**.
- **Film look:** grain, vignette, or both. This is set in **Settings → Edit**.
- **Clip editor:** in each clip's **Edit** panel you can:
  - fix any wrong caption words (the timing is kept)
  - get **Ideas** for other hook titles from the AI
  - nudge the start and end
- **Cover image:** each clip also gets a cover/thumbnail image with the title set big, ready for Shorts and Reels.
- **Smart caption spot:** **Auto** puts the captions just under the speaker's chin, or above the face if it sits low in the frame.
- **System check:** **Settings → System** checks everything ClipForge needs and says how to fix anything missing.
- **Exports:** **Download all** gives you a .zip with every clip, its .srt subtitles, and a `captions.txt` file of titles and post captions.

## First time

1. Double-click **Install.bat**. It takes about 5–15 minutes and downloads:
   - Python packages
   - ffmpeg
   - deno
   - HyperFrames
   - OmniRoute
   - Ollama, plus a model (qwen3:8b) if you don't have one yet
2. Double-click **Start.bat**. It starts Ollama and OmniRoute, then opens ClipForge in your default browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765. App Hub should start it with `--no-browser` so nothing pops outside the hub. `--port 0` takes the first free port and the process prints `CLIPFORGE_PORT=`.

After that, you only need Start.bat.

## Using it

- **Link or file:** paste a link and click **Make clips**. You can also drag a video onto the box, or paste the full path of a video on your PC.
- **Before you start:** choose how many clips you want, their length, and the caption style.
- **While it works:** clips appear as they finish. Each one has a virality score and the AI's reason for picking it.
- **Edit** a clip to:
  - change its hook title
  - nudge the start or end
  - re-render it
- **Caption** copies the post caption with hashtags.
- **Settings** has the colours, what the edit does, and the AI setup. The AI tab shows whether OmniRoute and Ollama are running.
- **Output:** clips are saved in `data\projects\<id>\clips\`. The **Open folder** button takes you there.

## OmniRoute

OmniRoute is an AI router with an OpenAI-compatible API. You can connect it to free and paid providers, and to Ollama, in its dashboard at http://localhost:20128. ClipForge sends its requests to the model `auto`, and OmniRoute picks one.

- To pin a model or combo, set it in **Settings → AI**.
- If you turned on `REQUIRE_API_KEY` in OmniRoute, paste the key there too.

## Updating

When Claude leaves you a `ClipForge-update.zip`, double-click **Update.bat**. It finds the zip and installs it. Your clips and settings are kept.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Sign in to confirm you're not a bot":** log in to YouTube in Firefox, close Firefox, and try again.
- **Captions look basic:** HyperFrames couldn't render. Run Install.bat again, or put your Chrome path in **Settings → Advanced**.
- **Slow transcription:** without an NVIDIA GPU, Whisper runs on the CPU. Choose `base` in **Settings → AI** for more speed.

The folder can be moved anywhere. Every path inside it is relative.
